Moonbound Privacy Policy

Developer: Space Forge · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Moonbound is a rocket game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we don't collect any personal information, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game stores

Your progress (save games, upgrades, records and settings such as sound and graphics) is stored only on your device, inside the app. It never leaves your phone. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, this progress is deleted.

What we collect

Nothing. Moonbound:

  • has no accounts or sign-in;
  • shows no adverts;
  • contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools;
  • does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files;
  • does not connect to the internet while you play.

Google Play

You download Moonbound through Google Play, which is run by Google. Google may collect information about your device and downloads under its own privacy policy, which we don't control. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Moonbound is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what happens to your information (for example, if adverts are added), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
